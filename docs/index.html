    How it works

    The AI never gets the last word.

    Small AI models make mistakes. So every tool splits the work, and each part does what it is best at. The same three colours show up in every run above.

    AI

    AI model

    Reads, understands, writes

    Reads photos, understands job ads and drafts messages. It runs on your laptop with Ollama and Google's free Gemma 4 model.

    In the German Letter ExplainerReads a phone photo of an official letter, word by word.

    Code

    Code

    Checks, finds, counts

    Plain Python checks that every quote is really in your file, finds every date and euro amount, and does all the maths.

    In the German Letter ExplainerFinds every deadline and amount, and counts the days left.

    You

    You

    Decide what matters

    You approve the plan, the facts and the final text at clear checkpoints. Nothing is ever sent for you.

    In the German Letter ExplainerYou read what the letter means and decide what to do next.

    Private by designThe AI runs on your own computer, so your CV, letters and notes never leave it.
    Free to runEvery model is free and open. No account, no API key, no subscription.
    Open sourceAll code, tests and guides are public on GitHub. The code of tools 02 to 07 is MIT. The tracker, guides and images are CC BY-NC 4.0: free to use, not to sell.
