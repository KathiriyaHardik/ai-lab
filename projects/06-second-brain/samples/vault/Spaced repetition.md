# Spaced repetition

Reviewing something just before you would forget it makes the memory last much longer. This is the idea behind the forgetting curve that Hermann Ebbinghaus described.

My review intervals: after 1 day, 3 days, 7 days and 14 days. If I get a card wrong, it goes back to the 1-day interval.

I use the free app Anki for this. See [[German vocabulary routine]].
