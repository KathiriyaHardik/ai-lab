# Idea: German letter helper

A small tool: take a photo of an official German letter and get a short explanation in simple English: who sent it, what to do and by when.

Many people around me struggle with letters about taxes, registration and insurance. The deadline is what matters most.

It has to run locally, because these letters contain very personal data.
