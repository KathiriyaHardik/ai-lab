# Interview preparation

Answer behaviour questions with the STAR method: Situation, Task, Action, Result.

Prepare 5 stories before every interview: a success, a failure, a conflict, teamwork and something I learned quickly.

Research the company: products, recent news, and the team I would join. Prepare 3 questions to ask at the end.
