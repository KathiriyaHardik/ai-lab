# Anmeldung checklist

After moving to a new flat you have to register your address (Anmeldung) within 14 days.

Bring: passport, and the Wohnungsgeberbestätigung, a form signed by the landlord.

Book the appointment at the Bürgerbüro online early, because free slots are often weeks away.
