# Book notes: Atomic Habits

Four rules for a new habit: make it obvious, make it attractive, make it easy, make it satisfying.

Habit stacking: attach the new habit to one you already have. My example: after my morning coffee, I do 10 Anki cards.

Never miss twice. Missing one day is an accident, missing two days is the start of a new habit.
