# German vocabulary routine

Every day I add 20 new words to Anki and do my reviews on the S-Bahn, which takes 25 minutes each way.

I write every word with its article and plural, for example "der Vertrag, die Verträge". Example sentences from real letters and job ads work better than word lists.

Goal: B2 level by June 2027. Related: [[Spaced repetition]]
